## Create: Dragons Plus 1.9.2

### Update
* Add **Fragile Fluid Tank** and **Levitite Fragile Fluid Tank**-Fluid Tanks break on impact and apply effects depending on fluid inside. They appear when Create: Simulated is installed.
```
Currently valid fluid: Potion, Tea, Water, Lava, Dye Fluid.
Lava can wipe snow, melten ice, lit block, spread fire ("spread fire" is default on in config) and set fire on entity. 
Dye Fluid can color entity and block.
Water extinguish fire and solidate concrete powder.

Effect of Dragon Breath Fluid will be implemented later.

For modpacker: Lava effect and Water effect is customizable via DataMaps. For more customization please check newly added datamaps/configs.
```

Ponder will be added later.
